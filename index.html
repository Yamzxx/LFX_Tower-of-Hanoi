RECURSION hanoi(n, src, dst, aux) is the classic divide-and-conquer solver.
It calls itself twice for each n > 0, with a decreasing sub-problem:
  ① hanoi(n-1, src, aux, dst) — clear the top n-1 disks
  ② move disk n from src → dst
  ③ hanoi(n-1, aux, dst, src) — restack on top
Base case: n=0 → return immediately. Total moves = 2ⁿ − 1 (optimal).


ITERATION render() redraws the canvas by iterating over the three pegs and the disk stack within each peg. The auto-play timer iterates through the pre-computed move list at a configurable interval.
